Privacy Policy & Engineering Confidentiality

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Ningbo Shitu Machinery Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (“Ningbo Shitu Machinery”, “we”, “us”) operates the website hujianhua.shop. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to respond to your inquiry and to quote and produce your order:

  • Contact details you provide through the inquiry form or by email — such as your name, company name, email address, phone number and message.
  • Engineering files you send us for quotation — such as CAD / STEP drawings, specifications, photos and samples.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate this website, such as browser type and pages visited. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotations, confirm samples, produce and deliver your order, and communicate with you about production and delivery. We do not use your information for any unrelated purpose.

3. Engineering Drawing Confidentiality

All CAD / STEP drawings, specifications and samples you send us are treated as confidential. They are used only for quotation and production of your project, are accessible only to staff involved in that project, and are not shared, sold, published or used for any other customer.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only where necessary to fulfil your order — for example with a freight forwarder to arrange delivery — or where required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention

We keep your contact details and project files only as long as needed for the business purpose for which they were provided, or as required for legal record-keeping.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data you have provided to us. Please contact us at the email address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
