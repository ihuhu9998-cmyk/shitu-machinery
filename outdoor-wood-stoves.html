Frequently Asked Questions for OEM Stove Buyers

Clear answers on custom CAD prototyping, thermal durability, and export logistics.

Q1: Can you manufacture outdoor wood stoves based on our own CAD drawings?

Yes. Send us your STEP, DXF, or PDF drawings. Because we operate our own mold-making, stamping, bending, and welding workshops, we can prototype custom stove dimensions, damper structures, and laser-cut logos within 10–14 days.

Q2: How do you prevent deformation of stainless steel tent stoves under high heat?

We incorporate reinforced internal stiffening ribs stamped via our hydraulic and mechanical presses, combined with controlled gauge thickness (1.0 mm–1.5 mm 304 stainless steel) to minimize thermal warping during prolonged burning.
