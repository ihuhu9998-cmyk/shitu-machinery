Step 02 · High-Volume Stamping

Frequently Asked Questions for Global OEM Buyers

Direct answers on CAD file formats, rapid prototyping, and export regions.

Q1: What file formats do you accept for custom sheet metal quotes?

We accept 3D CAD models (STEP, STP, IGES, SolidWorks) as well as 2D engineering drawings (DXF, DWG, PDF) with material grade, thickness, finish, and estimated order quantity.

Q2: Can you support small-batch prototyping before mass production?

Yes. We support single-piece or small-batch functional prototypes (5–7 days for sheet metal parts) so your engineers can verify fitment and strength before committing to volume production.

Q3: Which export markets do you supply?

We export directly from Ningbo Port under FOB, CIF, or DDP terms to North America (USA & Canada), Europe (Germany, UK, France, Netherlands, Scandinavia & across the EU), Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
